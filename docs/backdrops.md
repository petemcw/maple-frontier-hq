# Backdrops

Every image the Today backdrop needs, each with its prompt. Decided in ADR-0031. There are 316 images: 111 in the base set, 16 weatherless backdrops, 182 holiday backdrops and 7 moon sprites.

`docs/backdrops.csv` holds the same list with each full prompt (style preamble included), ready for a batch run. This page is for reading.

## How to make them

1. **Reference photos.** Take 3 to 5 photos of the house from the street, with no people, cars or house numbers in them. Attach them to every call.
2. **One base per season.** Make `{season}-clear-daylight` first for each season and approve it. Then make every other cell in that season by editing its base ("make it overcast", "twilight, lamps lit"), so the house and framing never drift.
3. **Lock the style.** Once 3 to 5 backdrops are approved, attach them as style references for the rest.
4. **Curate.** Make about 4 drafts per cell and keep one. Export at 5040×2160 (21:9) as WebP. Moons are square transparent PNGs.
5. **Record it in the manifest.** For each kept image, set its text tone (light or dark text over the upper left) and its focus point (where the house sits, as x and y from 0 to 1). Phones crop around the focus point.

Order of work, if you want the backdrop live early: the base set's daylight and dark cells first, then the weatherless set, then golden hour and twilight, then holidays as each one comes up. Anything missing falls back: holiday to base, base to weatherless, weatherless to a plain background.

## Style preamble

Every backdrop prompt starts with this:

> A soft storybook painting in gouache: flat areas of color, gentle paper texture, simple rounded shapes and fine brush edges. A wide 21:9 panorama. The house from the reference photos sits right of center, seen from the street at eye level, with its yard and trees around it. Keep the upper-left third of the picture as open sky with little detail, so text can sit there. No people, animals, cars, text, logos, signatures or moon.

## Rules the list follows

- **Weather groups** come from Open-Meteo's hourly WMO code:

  | Group | WMO codes |
  | --- | --- |
  | Clear | 0, 1 |
  | Partly cloudy | 2 |
  | Windy | 0 to 2 with wind over 19 mph |
  | Overcast | 3 |
  | Fog | 45, 48 |
  | Rain | 51 to 63, 80, 81 |
  | Heavy rain | 65, 82 |
  | Freezing rain | 56, 57, 66, 67 |
  | Snow | 71 to 77, 85, 86 |
  | Thunderstorm | 95 to 99 |

- **Light phase** comes from the sun's elevation, rising or setting: daylight above 20°, golden hour from 20° down to 8°, twilight from 8° down to −6°, dark below that.
- **Golden hour** exists only where the sun shows: clear, partly cloudy and windy. Overcast, fog, rain, heavy rain and thunderstorm cells use daylight then.
- **Season** is by month: December to February winter, March to May spring, June to August summer, September to November autumn. Snow and freezing rain always use winter art, so they appear only in winter. Winter has no thunderstorm.
- **Holidays** win all day, midnight to midnight. Each has a day image (daylight, golden hour and twilight) and a dark image, for each weather group likely in its month. St. Patrick's Day, Easter, Halloween and Veterans Day include snow and freezing rain, painted in their own season (snow on autumn leaves for Halloween). Holiday images get no moon.
- **Moon.** Dark images for clear, partly cloudy and windy leave sky free for the moon. Home HQ places the moon sprite at the spot the manifest records, between moonrise and moonset. A new moon shows nothing.
